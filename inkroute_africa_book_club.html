OUR STORY

InkRoute Africa was founded in 2021 by a group of passionate readers who wanted to celebrate diverse narratives, classic African literature, and worldwide bestsellers under one warm umbrella.

What started as a 5-person coffee shop club has now grown into a vibrant online and offline community spanning over 12 countries.
